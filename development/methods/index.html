What was counted, and what could only be estimated

The successful natural-language campaign and the later Lean autoformalization were different processes. They are counted separately here, with measured usage separated from inferred hidden reasoning.

Scope boundary

The solution estimate begins with the successful second-half-of-June campaign and ends with the repaired natural-language proof on June 26. It excludes the earlier GPT-5.2 and GPT-5.4 attempts, as requested. It also excludes the June 27–28 attempt to compare Roe’s and Turturean’s presentations, because that comparison remained unfinished and was not a dependency of either Lean proof.

The formalization estimate begins June 29. It includes Turturean’s Codex–ChatGPT Pro workflow and later Fable-led continuation, plus Roe’s independent Fable-led workflow.

Definitions and reproducibility

Processed tokens
Fresh input, cache creation or cache write, cache read, and output. Codex’s total_tokens already includes input and output; cached input is a subset of input and is never added twice.
Non-cache subtotal
Fresh input plus cache creation/write plus output. This removes cache reads, but it is still not a count of unique semantic content.
Hidden-reasoning estimate
A sensitivity range, not provider telemetry. OpenAI documents that reasoning tokens exist and count against output limits, but its public material does not expose historical hidden-reasoning counts for ChatGPT Pro conversations.
